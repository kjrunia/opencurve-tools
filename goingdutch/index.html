The Going Dutch Calculator

This will calculate who pays whom how much after a lovely weekend with the whole gang, assuming all costs are shared evenly.

Enter the names and for each the total cost they spent. No information is stored. No information is sent to anyone. Just you. On the screen.

This is just a side project for the author to teach himself some JavaScript programming. Do have a look at our main website for some more maths and physics.
